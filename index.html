001  —  Meet the artist

Fig. 01 — Paris / New York

Hi, I’m Menna — a storyteller and spatial experience designer.

I specialize in crafting immersive environments where stories transcend the page and unfold within space. I bring design concepts to life through thorough research and a deep commitment to transforming ideas into tangible experiences.

My work evokes emotion, sparks curiosity, and invites audiences to step inside the story. From concept to completion, I oversee every aspect of the design process — always eager to learn and grow as a human, designer, and artist.
